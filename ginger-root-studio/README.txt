Rebuild (Python 3 with reportlab, pillow, pypdf; poppler for pdffonts):
  cd build-scripts
  python3 build_book1.py ../Ginger_Root_Studio_Brand_Business_Workbook.pdf
  python3 build_books23.py seasonal ../Ginger_Root_Studio_Seasonal_Collections.pdf
  python3 build_books23.py sacred ../Ginger_Root_Studio_Sacred_Seasons.pdf
  python3 make_ase.py ../Ginger_Root_Studio_Master_Palette.ase
  python3 validate.py      (expects the outputs in ../out/ - adjust OUT at the top of the file)

The concept boards and motif studies are original vector drawings generated by these scripts.
They are development references, not Alisha's artwork and not verified seamless tiles.
